Observed wavelength = rest wavelength × (1 + z). The axis is logarithmic, so redshifting slides the spectrum sideways without changing its shape. Violet shading marks where the rest-frame UV lands; dashed, faded filters sit entirely blueward of Lyα, so they see no flux from the galaxy (drop-outs). The spectrum is an illustrative model in arbitrary fλ with exaggerated line widths and a simple intergalactic-medium absorption (Lyα forest and Lyman limit). Filter curves are smoothed top-hats built from approximate band edges, not the official throughput tables. UVEX shows its two imaging bands (FUV and NUV). HWO has no defined filter set yet, so its three bars are the notional UV, visible and near-infrared channels, with cutoffs that are still under discussion. Lookback time assumes flat ΛCDM, H₀ = 70, Ωm = 0.3.
